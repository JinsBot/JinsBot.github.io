FLIP SIDE

A turn-based deck-building game with customizable weapon coins.

Customize both sides of weapon coins and scatter them across a grid-based field. Use items and cards to counter the boss’s attacks and defeat it.

Implementation example: selection and cancellation

Problem

During development, cancelling a selection could leave the visible coin and the next selectable coin out of sync.

Correction

I corrected the return animation and slot order together, so the visible result and the next input stayed aligned.

I also updated preparation-phase slot clicks and input bindings after a teammate’s interaction-interface refactor.
